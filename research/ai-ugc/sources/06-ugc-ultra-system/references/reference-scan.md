# Reference Ad Scan

Use when a competitor/winning ad, video, or freeze frames are provided.

A video should be read as frames if possible. Prioritize:
- first frame;
- first clear product appearance;
- hero/demo beat;
- payoff/final frame;
- roughly one frame per second if extraction is available.

Extract a short **BONES SHEET** in this order:

1. **Mechanism + confidence** — e.g. `accidental discovery — 0.90`.
2. **Hook** — exact shape/function: question, confession, correction, flex, surprise, embarrassment, etc.
3. **Beat map** — what happens every ~2 seconds; timestamp first product reveal.
4. **Camera** — front/rear, height, handheld/propped, framing, number of cuts.
5. **Creator** — visible age band, hair, skin, clothing, delivery; describe, do not identify.
6. **Product** — what is visible, text that is actually readable, how it is held/lit.
7. **Environment/light** — room, props, light source, which side of face is brighter.
8. **Overlay timing** — on-screen text/captions and when they appear.
9. **Sound** — room tone, music, spoken language, SFX.
10. **Why it holds attention** — one sentence tied to a visible structural choice, not vague praise.

## Keep the bones, swap the parts

Usually keep:
- mechanism;
- hook shape;
- beat timing;
- reveal timing;
- camera logic;
- pacing.

Usually swap:
- creator identity;
- product/brand;
- exact wording;
- room details;
- proof beat as required by the new product.

Never copy a competitor creator’s face or raw footage. Treat the reference as a structural template.
