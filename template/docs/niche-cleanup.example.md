## Rule out

- **C1** Not about the niche's subject: another animal, the creator's own life, a
  product unboxing with no subject in it. Examples: `tiktok:7412345678901234567`
  (@dog.days, "beach day with my pup"), `tiktok:7412345678901234999` (@vlog.life).
- **C2** Giveaways, follow-for-follow, and reposted memes with no text of their own.
  Examples: `tiktok:7412345678901235000` (@free.stuff).
  - 2026-10-26: also fan art, from your words: "fan art is never useful to us".

## Study

- **S1** Slideshows that list numbered signs, tips or mistakes, with 100,000 views or
  more. Examples: `tiktok:7498765432109876543` (@vet.notes, "5 signs your cat is in pain").

## Keep (never rule out)

- **K1** Any post by an account that posts for an app in the niche, whatever its subject.

## Ask

  list it in the report.
