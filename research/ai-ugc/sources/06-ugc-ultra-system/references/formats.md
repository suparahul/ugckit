# Formats

Pick a **mechanism** and a **production format**.

## Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Why it works | Good fit |
|---|---|---|
| Shocked reaction + demo | reaction opens a question; demo answers it | apps/products with one impressive action |
| Product-as-gameplay | the demo itself is entertainment | visual, weird, satisfying core loops |
| Notification punchline | embarrassment/comedy creates shares | products with screenshot-worthy copy |
| Mascot / character engine | recurring character compounds recognition | brands willing to build around a character |
| What-worked-for-me note | peer recommendation feels native | fitness, habit, wellness, productivity |
| Spot-the-AI | question farms comments/dwell | AI UGC/creator services |
| Accidental discovery | viewer feels late to something useful | tools discovered mid-task |
| Trend-template at volume | distribution comes from variation volume | products that can support many versions |

## Production formats

### Talking-head (TH)
Face carries the ad. Product may flash once, late. Hook → proof → stop.

### How-to (HT)
One job, three beats max: tap → result → why it matters. Use real locked UI only.

### Before / after (BA)
Same person, room, clothes, and lighting. Change behavior/product state, not identity or appearance.

### Confession (CF)
Small true-sounding admission → the product is the fix already in use. Quiet, close, specific.

### Macro demo
Product/action first. Useful when physics or tactile behavior is the hook.

### Mirror selfie
Casual body/room context with creator still visibly locked.

### Over-the-shoulder
Face/identity remains present enough to preserve continuity while product UI is central.

### Front-cam + screen proof
Creator remains in frame while the app/result is shown. Good for one-face how-to cards.

## Batch distribution for Mode B

50 cards exactly:
- TH 01-15
- HT 16-30
- BA 31-40
- CF 41-50

Do not write 15 hooks that are the same sentence with one noun swapped. Rotate the job the product does: start, stick, recover, prove, simplify, hide friction, show the win, remove a step, prevent failure, reveal progress.
