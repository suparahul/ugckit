# Quality bar (not the product)

This is a sample of the bar. Use it only if they did not send a product. If they sent a photo and a line, ignore Focus Loop and lock what they sent.

One-liner: Focus Loop, a daily focus timer.

```
CREATOR LOCK
name: Maya
face: mid-20s, round face, light brown skin, dark hair in a low bun, faint mole by the left eye, no glam makeup
voice: medium, a little fast, drops the last word, says "okay so" once
lighting: phone front-cam, window camera-left, cool daylight, no ring light
wardrobe: grey hoodie, hair in the bun
room: bedroom, plain wall, unmade pillow edge in the corner
camera: front cam, chest height, phone in hand from frame 0
negative: no second person, no face swap, no age change, no makeup change, no new room, no new outfit

PRODUCT LOCK
app: Focus Loop
one line: a daily focus timer
screen: dark timer, single ring, 25:00
colors: near-black background, one warm orange ring
must show: the ring starting
must not: a second app, a fake streak number, text that is not on the photo
```
